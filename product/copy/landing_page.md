## Long hero version

### Headline

**Grassroot Hopper is looking for a technical cofounder who finishes what others start**

### Body copy

This is not a generic job application and it is not a networking form pretending to be one.

Grassroot Hopper is early. Julien brings operator credibility, real skin in the game, willingness to be CEO, and the energy to push something into existence. What is missing is a technical counterpart who does not just love prototypes and new tools, but likes the dense middle and end of product building too — and is senior enough to own technical direction rather than wait to be managed.

If you have a real relationship with product closure — if you know how to take something messy, promising, and half-finished and keep going until it becomes real — this is probably for you.

If you mostly love the opening, the tooling, the demo, and the feeling of possibility, but lose interest once continuity and polish begin, this is probably not for you.

## What Grassroot Hopper is

Grassroot Hopper is building tools and infrastructure around an open, local, cooperative vision. But this tool is narrower than the whole movement.

Right now, the immediate problem is simple:

- Julien should not be finding cofounders through awkward coffee chats and friend-convincing
- the project needs a serious inbound path
- the first real target is a technical cofounder who can complement Julien's blind spot

This funnel exists to create that path.

## What Julien brings

- Proven operator track record
- A profitable existing business
- Real founder risk and skin in the game
- CEO willingness
- High ambiguity tolerance
- Strong early-stage pressure, direction, and strategic repositioning

### Plainly

Julien is strong at the opening:

- framing the mission
- creating momentum
- shaping the map
- pushing the thing forward

## What is missing

Not "someone technical" in the vague sense.

More specifically:

- someone who closes loops
- someone who has finished products, not just started many
- someone who stays calm as complexity grows
- someone who does not need endless novelty to stay engaged
- someone who can turn movement into product
- someone who can make senior technical calls
- someone who can eventually manage and shape a development team

### Plainly

Julien is weaker in the long, orderly, continuity-heavy work that begins after the exciting opening phase.

That is the gap.

## What this role is

### CTO / Pragmatic Builder

A technical cofounder who:

- builds with AI-native leverage
- ships fast, but not sloppily
- knows how to survive the boring final 20%
- can keep a system coherent once it gets denser
- can challenge Julien without ego theater
- can own architecture and technical prioritization
- can grow into a real CTO role, not just stay a prolific developer

This person may already have a real career behind them and may be at the exact moment where cofounding finally makes sense.

They may not have the runway, CEO energy, or public founder pressure Julien brings. That is fine. The point is complementarity.

## Who should apply

You should apply if this sounds like you:

- you have built things that other people actually used
- you know what "finished enough to matter" means
- you can work under ambiguity without romanticizing chaos
- you enjoy turning messy momentum into something stable
- you like AI as leverage, not as a substitute for judgment
- you want to cofound, not consult from the sidelines
- you are senior enough to own technical direction

## Who should not apply

This is probably not for you if:

- you mainly collect side projects and never close them
- you love tooling more than product
- you need a highly specified roadmap before moving
- you want title, status, or certainty more than real responsibility
- you need to be convinced to care
- you are looking for a normal hiring process
- you want to code, but not to make senior calls or lead others

### Hard version

If your main evidence is "I shipped 18 repos in a month" but none of them became real, stable, or relied upon, this is probably not the right fit.

## What the process looks like

- serious application
- AI-assisted scoring that is advisory, not final
- human review by Julien
- shortlist
- direct conversation
- small real-world collaborative test if there is signal

If the work is non-trivial, it should be paid.
