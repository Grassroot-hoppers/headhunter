# Question Bank

## Purpose

Single place to maintain application questions and alternates.

## Canonical v1 question set

This file mirrors the current product spec and marks what is:

- **scored**
- **informational**
- **deferred / later**

The most important correction in the current version is this:

for the CTO path, the tool should screen for **closure**, **finished-product evidence**, and **senior technical ownership**, not just prototype energy.

## Core questions

### Scored core questions

1. **Role track selection**  
   Why: route scoring and follow-up

2. **What about Grassroot Hopper makes you want to apply now, specifically?**  
   Why: intrinsic motivation, project understanding

3. **What is the strongest proof that you ship? Link to something you built, ran, fixed, organized, or launched. What was your actual contribution, and did it actually get finished or used?**  
   Why: execution, ownership, closure

4. **Tell me about a time a tool/system didn’t do what you wanted, so you bypassed/broke/rebuilt it.**  
   Why: high agency

5. **If we build for 3 months and market ignores it, what do you do Monday morning?**  
   Why: ambiguity comfort, execution bias

6. **What AI trend is overrated and why?**  
   Why: independent judgment

7. **Tell me about a time you challenged a strong opinion from a founder, lead, or group. What happened?**  
   Why: backbone, communication, low ego

8. **Describe a project where the brief was messy or incomplete. How did you create clarity and move it forward?**  
   Why: ambiguity, ownership

9. **What kind of founder partnership do you work best in, and what kind tends to fail?**  
   Why: founder fit

10. **What level of time and risk can you actually commit in the next 6 months? Be concrete.**  
    Why: commitment realism

11. **Tell me about a time you made something simpler instead of more impressive.**  
    Why: pragmatism

12. **What is the most boring repeated task in early-stage company building that you would automate first here, and why?**  
    Why: leverage instincts

### Informational core questions

- location / timezone
- relevant links
- collaboration preferences

## CTO / Builder track questions

### Scored CTO questions

13-B. **Show a product, system, script, automation, internal tool, or repo that demonstrates how you work. What trade-off did you make to ship fast, and what made it actually finished enough to matter?**

14-B. **When have you built with unclear requirements and missing resources, but still produced something useful and carried it far enough that other people could rely on it?**

15-B. **Tell me about a time the exciting prototype phase was over and the work became slower, denser, or more boring. How did you keep moving until it was done?**

16-B. **Describe a product or system that became more complex over time. What did you do to keep it coherent once the novelty wore off?**

17-B. **Tell me about a senior technical decision you owned that affected other builders or the product direction. How did you make the call, and how did you get people aligned?**

18-B. **If Grassroot Hopper had three developers in a year, what kind of CTO would you be for them?**

### What these CTO questions are trying to catch

- close-the-loop behavior
- finish discipline
- comfort with complexity as it grows
- willingness to stay after the opening
- real product maturity instead of repo-count theater
- technical ownership
- leadership potential
- ability to make senior calls without waiting to be managed

## Community / Ops track questions

### Scored Community / Ops questions

13-C. **Tell me about a workshop, event series, community, or operational system you helped run. How did you get people to show up or stay engaged?**

14-C. **Describe a time you created structure for a messy human process. What changed after your intervention?**

## Follow-up interview questions

### CTO / Builder follow-ups

- What did you finish that you are proudest of, and what almost made you stop before it was done?
- Tell me about a product you inherited in a messy state. What did you do first?
- What does "finished enough to matter" mean to you?
- When does your perfectionism help, and when does it waste time?
- What kind of founder makes it easier for you to do your best work?
- Tell me about a time you had to choose between technical elegance and shipping pressure for a team, not just for yourself.
- What kind of developer do you enjoy leading, and what kind do you struggle to manage?

### Community / Ops follow-ups

- What process do you naturally create when things get messy?
- How do you keep people aligned when energy drops after launch?
- What is your version of "closing the loop" in human systems?

## Deferred / later questions

- required mini-task before shortlist
- public proof alternatives for candidates with little online footprint
- calibration alternates if the CTO track over-selects for polished narrators
