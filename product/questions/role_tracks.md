# Role Tracks

## Purpose

Define the role-track framing, weights, and wording used in the landing page, form, and dashboard.

## Current v1 priority

The current working priority is:

1. **CTO / Pragmatic Builder** first
2. **Community / Ops Architect** second or later if it adds too much scope too early

Why:

- the minimum viable founding core appears to be **Julien + close-the-loop technical cofounder**
- finance is likely fractional/advisory rather than a cofounder track
- ops/admin support is likely freelance or part-time rather than a cofounder track

## Universal bar for meaningful roles

Every serious role should be screened for:

- high agency
- judgment under ambiguity
- AI leverage
- fast good-enough shipping
- ability to avoid hallucination loops and perfectionist waste

## Tracks

### CTO / Pragmatic Builder

**What this role is**

A technical cofounder who does not just prototype fast, but knows how to close loops, survive the boring final 20%, turn messy product motion into something real people can use, and make CTO-level calls without needing Julien to manage them technically.

**Founder complement**

Julien is strong at the opening: pressure, positioning, optionality, direction, and narrative. This role is strong in the mid/late game: continuity, technical product maturity, stabilization, finishing, and technical leadership.

**Ideal signals**

- has finished products, not just started many things
- can keep moving as complexity increases
- likes making systems coherent after the novelty phase
- comfortable with AI-native building, but not intoxicated by tools for their own sake
- can challenge Julien without ego theater
- can make senior technical decisions and get alignment from other builders
- can grow into leading a small development team rather than staying only an individual contributor

**Anti-signals**

- many repos, demos, or experiments with little evidence of closure
- excitement about novelty, weak appetite for maintenance
- wants to invent forever, resists simplifying and shipping
- needs a founder to constantly manage their execution discipline
- strong engineer, weak technical owner
- wants to code, but not to own direction or lead others

**Example titles**

- Technical Cofounder
- CTO
- Founding Engineer
- Product Engineer
- AI Systems Builder
- Technical Product Lead
- Engineering Lead

### Community / Ops Architect

**What this role is**

A human-systems builder who can run workshops, communications, community operations, and structured follow-through when Grassroot Hopper reaches the point where those functions become core.

**Current status**

This role is real, but may be secondary or later relative to the technical cofounder track.

**Ideal signals**

- can create structure for messy human systems
- closes loops reliably
- strong writing and communication
- good event/process judgment
- can use AI well for preparation and ops acceleration

**Anti-signals**

- socially polished but operationally weak
- high enthusiasm, low repeatability
- good taste, poor follow-through
- wants title or identity more than real responsibility

**Example titles**

- Founding Operator
- Community Lead
- Program Lead
- Operations Architect
- Workshop / Community Producer
